## The Elevator Drop Through the Earth's Crust

*Geology Fact: It takes thousands of years for solid rock to turn into topsoil!*

**Organic Matter:** Leaves, twigs, and plant bits break down to feed the soil.

**Topsoil:** Dark, rich soil full of nutrients. Great for plant roots!

**Subsoil:** Lighter soil with fewer nutrients. Some small rocks and minerals.

**Small Rocks & Pebbles:** Broken-down pieces of rock.

**Large Rocks:** Bigger rocks broken down over time.

**Bedrock:** Solid rock deep underground.

## The Microbe Special Forces

Every microbe has a specific job. Together, they turn waste into life-giving compost.

**Cellulolytic Bacteria (The Tough Recyclers):** Specialise in breaking down plant cell walls (like cellulose).

**Nitrogen Fixing Bacteria (The Element Fixers):** Convert atmospheric nitrogen.

**Algae & Cyanobacteria:** Photosynthetic microbes that add oxygen.

**Lactic Acid Bacteria:** Lower pH to preserve organic matter.

**Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria:** Make phosphorus available.

**Sulfur Oxidizing Bacteria:** Help detoxify compost.

**Fungi (The Mushrooms):** Nature's ultimate recyclers. They break down tough plant fibres like lignin.

**Protozoa (The Amoebas):** Microscopic predators that eat bacteria and release nutrients as they go.

**Nematodes (The Worms):** Microscopic roundworms that help control bacterial populations.

**Tardigrades:** The tough guys that help break down organic matter.

They may be invisible, but they are the true rulers of the garden!
