# Gumroad Cover Image Prompts — Wildroots Bundles

**Specs (from Gumroad's product editor):**
- Cover: horizontal, ≥ 1280×720 px (16:9), 72 DPI
- Thumbnail: square, ≥ 600×600 px (JPG/PNG/GIF)

**Workflow:** generate each at 16:9 (1920×1080 if your generator allows), keep the key subject
in the middle ~60% of the frame, then center-crop a square copy for the thumbnail. One artwork,
both slots.

**House style block (append to every prompt):**
> Hand-illustrated storybook style, warm earthy palette of ochre, moss green, parchment cream
> and amber lantern glow, soft watercolor-and-ink textures, gentle solarpunk optimism, cozy and
> slightly magical, no text, no words, no lettering, no watermark, center-weighted composition
> with generous margins.

*(NO TEXT in the art — Gumroad shows the title right beside it, and generated lettering always
mangles. If you want a wordmark later, overlay it yourself.)*

## 1. The Wildroots Storybook Trilogy ($9)
> Three illustrated hardcover storybooks standing upright in a sunlit vegetable garden, leaning
> against a weathered wooden fence post, sprouting seedlings and curious garden creatures
> (earthworm, ladybird, small bird) gathered around them as if listening to a story, a rusted
> iron gate half-open in the soft-focus background, golden afternoon light.

## 2. The Wildroots Field Guide Set ($12)
> A child's wooden clipboard with field journal pages, a pencil stub and a magnifying glass,
> lying on rich dark soil beside muddy gumboots and freshly foraged wild greens (nettle,
> purslane), a hand-drawn map peeking out underneath, dappled morning light through leaves,
> dirt smudges on the pages worn like badges of honor.

## 3. The Alchemist's Shelf ($19)
> An underground laboratory shelf carved into living earth with visible roots crossing the wall,
> five thick leather-bound tomes standing between glass flasks of glowing green and amber
> potions, bubbling gently, a brass stone-mill and a basket of sprouted grains at the shelf's
> end, lit by a swaying paper lantern, deep indigo shadows with warm ember highlights.

## 4. The Homestead Originals ($15)
> A rustic farmhouse kitchen table at golden hour viewed slightly from above, five vintage
> guidebooks fanned out among a stone flour mill, a bowl of sunset-orange egg yolks, sprigs of
> wild herbs and a jar of sprouting seeds, a window behind showing a thriving vegetable garden
> and chickens, flour dust hanging in the warm light.

## 5. (Later) The Complete Wildroots Library ($39)
> A great living tree in cross-section, its canopy a sunlit garden with children reading, its
> root system an underground laboratory of books, flasks and glowing mycelium threads, sixteen
> small books nested among the roots and branches like fruit, the full circle of soil to soul
> in one image, dawn light.
