## 1. The one-paragraph frame

The Wildroots food-literacy line taught *"the earth is a kitchen."* The Wild Pharmacy is
its **second wing**: *"the earth is a pharmacy."* Same world, two ages. Auntie Flora is the
storybook grandmother for the children (the food line). **Hexi** is the wry hedge-witch for
the grown stewards (this line). The whole angle rolls a real, lived apothecary practice into
Wildroots content, rooted in the Overberg and the fynbos natives, conservation-positive,
educational-not-medical.

## 8. Voice & safety rules (hard constraints)

- **Educational, not medical advice.** Always.
- **Safety framing = liability, not nannying.** She's not safety-*first* by temperament; the
  cautions column exists as a fence that survives an adversarial reader (her Defensibility-Read
  instinct applied to a herb list). Frame accordingly.
- **No em-dashes in her-voice / outgoing text** (she doesn't use them; any are AI artifacts —
  strip & resolve in her style). Also avoid "Not X. Y." antithesis fragments and rule-of-three
  triplets in her voice. See memory `no-em-dashes-author-style`, `sociable-systems-public-voice`.
- **Don't fabricate source URLs.** Use her real ones (the 11) or named reputable source-types.
- **Don't facilitate regulated synthetics** (modafinil/racetams etc.) — index them educationally,
  never source them.
- **SA/Overberg-rooted, conservation-positive.** Flag endangered species (cultivated-only).
- She works **in portfolio, not single-focus** — offer state-indexed task menus, never "pick one
  lane" (memory `liezl-works-in-portfolio-not-single-focus`). Completion fuels her despair-buffer.
