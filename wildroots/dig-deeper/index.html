🌿 The Steward's Caution (read before you forage)

Everything in Wildroots is educational storytelling, not medical advice. Never eat a wild plant unless it has been properly identified — foraging needs local knowledge and adult or expert supervision. Sprouting and food preservation require clean, safe handling. Nutrition content here supports curiosity; it does not replace guidance from a qualified health professional. The pharmacy may be planted beneath your feet, but wisdom is knowing which door to knock on.
