⛏ BARNYARD ARCADE

🌙 Dreamscape Forager

Drift and gather in a floating dream.

🎯 Objective

Glide through the dreamscape and collect as many glowing Dewdrops as you can for the highest score.

🎮 How to play
  • Press Spacebar or click to hop/flap upward — release to drift gently down.
  • Float through the Dewdrops to collect them and build your score.
  • Missing a dewdrop costs a few points, so glide with intention.
🕹 Controls

Click / Spacebar = hop
